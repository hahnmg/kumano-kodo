How the four days went

Walking times without breaks, measured by the clock rather than estimated.

  1. Day 1 · 13 May

    Takijiri → Takahara

    4 km, 400 metres of climbing in one go. Short, but the steepest section of the route.

    2:10 h
  2. Day 2 · 14 May

    Takahara → Chikatsuyu

    10 km in steady rain. From midday the path was a streambed; the gaiters made no difference.

    4:40 h
  3. Day 3 · 15 May

    Chikatsuyu → Hongu

    26 km, the longest stage. At Mikoshi pass the mist broke briefly; after that it was downhill.

    9:20 h
  4. Day 4 · 16 May

    Koguchi → Nachi

    14 km over the Ogumotori-goe. It ends at Nachi waterfall, 133 metres high.

    7:00 h
