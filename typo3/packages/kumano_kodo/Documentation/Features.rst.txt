Design
    Five themes in light and dark; colours, typefaces, spacing and logo as site
    settings; own stylesheet, script and templates.

Content
    Two containers and 14 content elements, galleries with a lightbox, video in
    an image's place, a route map and tables from the text editor.

Core extensions
    Forms with every element type and steps, login, search, news, the XML
    sitemap and structured data, all in the theme's own markup.

Accessibility
    WCAG 2.2 AA as the target: contrast checked in every theme and mode,
    keyboard use, large targets, marked external links, reduced motion.

Privacy
    Fonts from the site itself, YouTube and Vimeo only after the visitor's yes,
    no tracking and no cookies of the theme's own.

Languages and print
    English, German and Japanese throughout; pages print as content only, ink on
    white.

For developers
    Fluid components for other extensions, no fluid_styled_content, a manual,
    and checks for code, contrast, accessibility and size.

Demo and starter
    A travel journal of a four-day walk in three languages to look at, and five
    pages to start a site from.
