============
Introduction
============

Kumano Kodo is a theme for a travel journal: calm pages, large photos, one
accent colour. It comes as a theme and two content packages.

`kumano-kodo/sitepackage`
    The theme. It ships no content and installs empty.

`kumano-kodo/demo`
    The full trail journal in English, German and Japanese, to look at.

`kumano-kodo/starter`
    Five pages with placeholder texts, to start a site of your own.

What it covers is listed on the :ref:`start page <start>`.
