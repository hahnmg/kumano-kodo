============
Introduction
============

Kumano Kodo is a theme for a travel journal: calm pages, large photos, one accent colour. It comes as a theme and two content packages.

`kumano-kodo/sitepackage`
    The theme. It ships no content and installs empty.

`kumano-kodo/demo`
    The full trail journal in English, German and Japanese, to look at.

`kumano-kodo/starter`
    Five pages with placeholder texts, to start a site of your own.

The components, their states and the tokens behind them are documented in the design system's Storybook; Figma is
the design source.

What it covers
==============

*   Four themes (Caramel, Forest, Ocean, Violet), light and dark mode, all colours, typefaces and spacing as site
    settings.
*   Containers Section and Columns, and 13 content elements with a display option each.
*   Templates for indexed_search, felogin and form, and the sitemap of seo.
*   Structured data (WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList) and a title format with the website title.
*   Own icons for every element and record type, preview images for the page layouts.
